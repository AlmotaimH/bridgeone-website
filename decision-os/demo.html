    STOCK & SUPPLY PORTAL · REORDER & TRANSFERS

    Enough on the shelf, less cash on it

    Every item at every location gets a safety stock and a reorder point. Before ordering, the tool checks whether another location has extra to move.

    Settings

    Safety stock = service factor × daily demand variability × √lead time. Reorder point = daily demand × lead time + safety stock.

    Transfers before new orders

    Stock is moved only when a location has more than its target and another is below its reorder point, and only when the trip costs less than the margin at risk.
