About BridgeOne

BridgeOne Inc. is a business and management consulting firm based in the Greater Toronto Area. We help organizations bridge the gap between vision and execution, so plans turn into measurable results.

Founder Hossam Almotaim has spent nearly two decades leading business development and operations across retail, marketing, infrastructure and events in Canada, the MENA region, the GCC and Türkiye, including regional portfolios of 70+ stores and 550+ team members. He also writes Bridging the Gap, a LinkedIn newsletter that turns leadership research into everyday practice.
