Privacy policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

BridgeOne Inc. (“BridgeOne”, “we”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website and how we use it, in line with Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

What we collect

When you use our contact form, call or email us, or book a consultation, we collect the details you provide, such as your name, email address, company and message.

How we use it

We use your information only to respond to your enquiry and provide our services. We do not sell your personal information.

Service providers

We use trusted third parties to run this website, process forms and send email. They handle your information only on our behalf. Our newsletter, Bridging the Gap, is published on LinkedIn, and subscriptions are managed by LinkedIn under its own privacy policy.

Your choices

You can ask to access, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing info@bridge-one.ca.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email info@bridge-one.ca.
