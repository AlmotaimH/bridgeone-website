BridgeOne Decision OS

Better everyday decisions across every location

Decision OS is BridgeOne’s own decision system for businesses with several locations. It brings four portals, People, Stock & Supply, Franchise & Network and an Executive View, onto the data you already have, with clear weekly recommendations. Your managers approve them, and we measure the results with you every month.

Try the live demoBook a free call

The demo uses a fictional five-location business and runs entirely in your browser.

Built for businesses with more than one location

  • Retail chains and specialty stores
  • Cafés, restaurants and food service groups
  • Franchise networks, for franchisors and multi-unit franchisees
  • Service businesses with several branches or clinics

One system, four portals

Start with the portal you need and add the others when you're ready. They share the same data, so a sales forecast that sets tomorrow's roster also sets stock orders and the cash plan.

Our HR Portal and staff scheduling in one place, for HR and operations managers

People Portal

  • Staff rosters built from hourly demand and your labour target
  • Labour cost and overtime control within employment standards
  • HR answers and step-by-step decision guides, by province
  • Online HR requests with approval chains, target times and automatic checks against employee records
  • Letters and forms on your letterhead, ready to issue
For operations, buying and merchandising teams

Stock & Supply Portal

  • Reorder points and safety stock for every item and location
  • Transfers between locations before new orders
  • Markdown plans and promotion checks that protect margin
For franchisors, area managers and multi-unit operators

Franchise & Network Portal

  • Scorecards that compare every location
  • Go or no-go checks for new locations
  • Franchise fee and franchisee health checks
For the CEO, COO, CFO and owners

Executive View

  • Thirteen-week cash and buying plan
  • Monthly scorecard against the baseline
  • Decision log: who approved what, and what happened next

Every engagement starts with an assessment and baseline.

How it works

  1. AssessA free call, then a two to three week audit of your numbers and how decisions are made today.
  2. Set upWe connect the data you already keep, from your point of sale, payroll, inventory and accounting exports or spreadsheets, and set the rules with you.
  3. Decide weeklyManagers get recommendations they can approve, adjust or reject, each with a plain explanation of why.
  4. Measure monthlyWe compare results with the baseline, refine the rules and decide what to improve next.

What makes it different

Explainable

Every recommendation shows the reason and the numbers behind it. Nothing is a black box.

Your rules, your call

Your service levels, wage rules, cash floor and approval roles. People decide; the tools advise.

Starts with what you have

No new software to buy on day one. We begin with your exports and work alongside your payroll (ADP or another), point of sale and accounting.

Run with you

BridgeOne runs the assessment and works with your team directly, so the tools fit how your business really operates.
